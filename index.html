Help Us Take More People Fishing for Free

Strum City / Fallen Outdoors — free bowfishing trips for veterans, kids, and families who need it most.

What We Do

Every year we take 20–30 veterans, children, and disabled individuals out on the water completely free of charge. These are real guided bowfishing trips on Lake Conroe and surrounding waters — the same boats and experience our paying customers get.

We cover the fuel, arrows, gear, and guide time out of pocket. It matters, and we want to do more of it.

Why We Need Help

The demand is bigger than what we can fund ourselves. More people are asking for trips than we can currently take. Your donation directly covers:

  • Fuel for the boat
  • Arrows and replacement gear
  • Guide time and boat costs

$250–$400 typically covers one full free trip. Any amount helps us say yes to the next person.
